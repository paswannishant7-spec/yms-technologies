Notes from YMS Technologies

Ideas worth putting to work.

Read practical notes on planning mobile applications, preparing websites and connecting digital marketing to a clear user journey.

01

What belongs in the first version of an app?

Choose the task a user should be able to complete from start to finish. Sketch the states they’ll encounter when data is missing or a connection drops. A first release can be narrow in scope and still deliver a complete experience.

02

Why a faster website starts with its content

Identify what a visitor needs to see first and make that content available straight away. Give media an appropriate size and format, reserve space before it loads and let optional effects load after the essential information.

03

What to measure before starting a campaign

Identify the action that matters, the landing page that supports it and the signals that show where visitors get stuck. Agree how those signals will be collected before using them to make spending decisions.
